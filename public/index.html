How levels are estimated

Your volume maps to a level between 60 and 105 dB at the ear, then the cap and a limiter hold it down. Real hearing devices report their own output, so a shipping app would read that value and calibrate to it. Dose follows the NIOSH 85 dB / 3 dB rule.
